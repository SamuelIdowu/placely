# Placely — Product Brief

## Problem

Nigerian engineering students seeking mandatory SIWES (Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme) placements rely on WhatsApp groups, notice boards, and personal connections. Students without industry contacts are effectively shut out. Employers have no reliable way to post structured opportunities or vet candidates beyond word of mouth.

## Target User

- **Primary:** Nigerian university and polytechnic engineering students who need a SIWES placement.
- **Secondary (supply-side):** SMEs, startups, and mid-size companies willing to take on verified student interns.

## Value Proposition

A verified, two-sided marketplace where students build a profile and apply to real listings, and employers post opportunities, review applicants, and manage the process — replacing informal networks and one-way directories with an actual application pipeline.

## Key Research Findings

- **Closest competitor — SIWES Finder (MonoEd Africa):** a free, live directory of verified SIWES/IT placements, filterable by state and course, with student ratings. It is generalist (all courses) and browse-only — no employer accounts, and by its own FAQ, no application tracking yet ("future updates will include auto-apply and employer integration").
- **Adjacent competitors:** paid training providers (Learnovo, SkillBoost, HiiT, TechNeo) that guarantee a placement letter for a fee — a different model (they are the employer), solving the same anxiety but not a marketplace.
- **The wedge:** a true two-sided marketplace with employer accounts, structured listings, and real application tracking — not a directory. Engineering-only focus and deeper verification (school ID + employer CAC) sharpen this further.

## MVP Scope at a Glance

Verified student and employer profiles, internship listings, an application pipeline with status tracking, application-scoped messaging, and admin-run manual verification for both sides. Ratings, saved searches, and a CV builder are the first v1.x additions. Payments, gamification, real-time messaging infrastructure, and the previously-considered AI simulation/learning hub are explicitly deferred.
